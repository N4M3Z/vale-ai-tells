Hello,

If you placed an order with us last Tuesday, 15 September, it has taken longer than we promised. A warehouse system outage stopped us from picking and packing books that day. Every order placed on Tuesday was affected.

The outage began early Tuesday morning. The warehouse software stopped sending pick lists to the floor, so the team could not see which titles were on the shelf or which orders were ready to pack. The website stayed open and took payment as usual. The fault was in the link between our order system and the warehouse. Your account and your payment were not affected.

We restored the system on Wednesday morning. Engineers rebuilt the missed pick lists and matched each Tuesday order to the stock on hand. Where a title had sold out, we ordered a new copy from the publisher or refunded that line. Most parcels left on Wednesday and Thursday. The last of them left on Friday.

Here is what you should expect. Open the tracking link in your confirmation email. We added the tracking number when the parcel left the warehouse. From that point, allow two to four working days in the United Kingdom, or five to ten working days for an international order. Do not place the order again. You will not be charged a second time.

If tracking still shows no movement by Friday 25 September, reply to this email with your order number. We will trace the parcel that day. If the carrier has lost it, we will send a replacement or refund you in full, including delivery. If you would rather cancel, tell us before the parcel is out for delivery and we will refund the order.

We are sorry for the wait. Thank you for your patience.
