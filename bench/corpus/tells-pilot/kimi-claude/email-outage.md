Subject: An update on your recent order

Dear customer,

Thank you for ordering from Fable & Quill Books. We are writing to let you know about a delay affecting orders placed on Tuesday, 15 September.

Early that morning, the system that runs our warehouse suffered a serious outage. The software that receives orders, picks stock, and prints shipping labels stopped working, which meant we could not process or dispatch any orders for most of the day. No payment details or personal data were affected at any point. The problem was limited to our warehouse operations.

Our engineers restored the system late on Tuesday evening, and the warehouse team worked through Wednesday and Thursday to clear the backlog. Orders are now being picked, packed, and dispatched in the order they were received.

Here is what this means for you:

- Your order is confirmed and your books are reserved. Nothing has been cancelled.
- Your order will leave our warehouse within the next two working days.
- You will receive a dispatch email with tracking details as soon as your parcel is on its way.
- Delivery should arrive within three to five working days of dispatch, depending on your location.

You do not need to do anything. If your order has not been dispatched within five working days of this email, please reply to this message or contact our customer care team, and we will look into it straight away. If you would prefer to cancel your order for a full refund, just let us know and we will process it within one working day.

We know you were looking forward to your books, and we are sorry for the wait. As a small apology, we have added a 15% discount code to your account for your next order. You will find it on your account page.

Thank you for your patience and for supporting an independent bookshop.

Warm regards,
