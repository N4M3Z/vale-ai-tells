## Corvid Q3 review: strong sound, honest price

Corvid is not a household name, and the Q3 is only the third pair of over-ear wireless headphones the company has made. After two weeks of daily use on trains, at a desk, and on long walks, I can say this: the Q3 is a serious pair of headphones that costs less than it should.

### Sound

The Q3 uses 40mm dynamic drivers, and Corvid tuned them with restraint. The bass has weight without swallowing the midrange. Kick drums hit with a satisfying thump on electronic tracks, but vocals stay in front of the mix instead of drowning in low end. On acoustic recordings, guitars sound like wood and string, not like a synthesizer's idea of a guitar.

The treble is the one area where the tuning shows a slight preference for safety. Cymbals and hi-hats sound a touch rolled off, which makes long listening sessions easy on the ears but takes some sparkle out of brighter tracks. I noticed this most on jazz recordings, where a ride cymbal lost some of its shimmer. Most listeners will not care. Audiophiles with a careful ear might.

The soundstage is wider than I expected at this price. Instruments have room to breathe, and you can place a drummer behind and slightly to the left of a vocalist. It will not match a pair of open-back wired headphones, but for a closed wireless design it does well.

Active noise cancellation is competent rather than class-leading. It erases the low rumble of a train and the drone of an air conditioner almost completely. Higher-frequency sounds, like a colleague's keyboard clatter or a crying baby two rows back, get softened rather than removed. The transparency mode sounds natural enough for a quick conversation, though your own voice comes through slightly muffled.

### Comfort

At 254 grams, the Q3 sits in the comfortable middle of the weight range. The headband spreads pressure evenly, and the memory foam earpads are deep enough that my ears never touch the driver housing. I wore them for a four-hour stretch of writing and forgot they were on, which is the highest compliment I can give a pair of headphones.

Clamping force is on the gentle side. That is good news for glasses wearers, since the arms of my frames caused no hotspots. The tradeoff is that the Q3 shifts if you lean forward sharply or try to wear them during a workout. These are desk, commute, and couch headphones, not gym headphones.

The earpads run a little warm after an hour in a stuffy room. Nothing extreme, but on a summer day you will notice.

### Battery

Corvid claims 40 hours with noise cancellation on, and my testing came close. I averaged 37 hours across three full charge cycles at moderate volume. That puts the Q3 ahead of several competitors that cost twice as much.

A 10-minute charge over USB-C gives you roughly five hours of playback, which saved me twice when I forgot to plug in overnight. A full charge takes just under two hours. The Q3 also works passively with the included 3.5mm cable if the battery dies, though noise cancellation and the on-board controls stop working in wired mode.

### Value

Here is where the Q3 gets interesting. At $179, it undercuts the big-name flagships by a wide margin while matching them on battery life and getting most of the way there on sound and noise cancellation. The companion app is basic but functional, with a five-band equalizer and firmware update support. Build quality feels sturdy, with metal hinges and a folding design that fits into the included hard case.

What you give up: multipoint Bluetooth connects to only two devices at once, call quality is average in wind, and there is no support for the highest-resolution Bluetooth codecs. For most people, none of those gaps matters day to day.

### Verdict

The Corvid Q3 does not try to dazzle you. It plays music well, blocks most of the world's noise, stays comfortable for hours, and goes a week between charges. At $179, it is the easiest recommendation I have made this year. If you want the last word in noise cancellation, spend more. If you want honest, enjoyable sound and you would rather keep the difference in your pocket, buy the Q3.

**Score: 8.5/10**
