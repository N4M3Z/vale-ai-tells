Dear Customer,

We're writing to let you know that your order placed last Tuesday may arrive later than expected, and to explain why.

What happened

Last Tuesday, a fault in our warehouse management system stopped us from processing orders for most of the day. Our team spotted the problem early in the morning, but the fix took longer than we hoped. Any order placed that day sat in a queue until the system was restored on Wednesday evening.

What we've done

Since then, our warehouse team has been working through the backlog in order of purchase date. Most of the affected orders have now been packed and handed to the courier. Every affected order was given priority dispatch at no extra charge, and we've extended the cancellation window to 30 days for these purchases, in case you'd rather have a refund than wait.

What to expect

If your order has already shipped, you'll find updated tracking details in your account and in a separate confirmation email. If it hasn't shipped yet, it will leave our warehouse within the next two working days, and we'll email you the moment it does. We expect all backlogged orders to be delivered within five to seven working days of this email.

If your order was meant as a gift and the delay causes you a problem, reply to this email with your order number and we'll do our best to help, including upgrading your delivery where we can.

We're sorry for the wait. This outage was on us, not the couriers, and we've since added a backup process so a single system fault can't stall orders like this again.

Thank you for your patience, and happy reading.

The team at Willow & Page Books
